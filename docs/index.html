STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you buy

The right protection is the one you understand and can operate confidently.

Is Hypas specifically designed for RedM and VORP?

Yes. The public compatibility documentation lists RedM, VORP Core and oxmysql. Custom resources and economy integrations may need configuration or testing. Read installation requirements.

Will every suspicious player be automatically banned?

No. Hypas combines preventive checks, configurable enforcement and evidence-led investigation. Some clear unauthorised actions can justify immediate intervention; behavioural suspicion should be reviewed in context.

Can an anti-cheat guarantee that nobody will cheat?

No. Client software has inherent trust limitations. Hypas aims to reduce exploit opportunities, expose suspicious patterns and give staff evidence and containment tools. Secure server resources and sensible permissions remain essential.

What if I need help configuring it?

Use the public installation and configuration guides and the Hypas support Discord. Check the specific Tebex licence terms for support eligibility.

Can I switch from monthly to one-time access?

The two packages use separate entitlements. The monthly product listing says prior subscription payments do not convert into or reduce the one-time licence price. See Tebex for current terms before purchasing.
